# Product description

Themis is an AI-orchestrated transaction-dispute assistant that meets customers in RCS or SMS and carries the investigation forward for them. A customer can report an unfamiliar merchant in ordinary language; Themis finds related synthetic transactions, resolves merchant aliases, gathers structured evidence, classifies the claim, and applies deterministic financial-safety policy before proposing an outcome or escalating for human review.

The experience combines a conversational AgentCore Runtime with governed tools behind AgentCore Gateway and Cedar policy. Reports and evidence are retained for review, customers can receive channel and email updates, and operations teams can inspect cases and emerging merchant patterns in a dashboard.

The differentiator is institutional learning without unsafe autonomy. Merchant intelligence persists across cases, so a later dispute involving the same merchant can reuse prior aliases and research. The agent never touches a real bank account, never moves money, and cannot bypass the policy layer: ambiguous evidence and higher-risk outcomes go to a person.

The hackathon build uses synthetic customers, accounts, and transactions. Its strongest end-to-end proof is the local composition test; live AWS channel identities and several managed-service integrations still require deployment-time verification.
